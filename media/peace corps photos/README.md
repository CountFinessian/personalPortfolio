# Jacob Williams — Peace Corps Volunteer, Thailand

**Role:** Youth Mentor & English Teacher  
**Location:** Rural community, Southern Thailand  
**Duration:** ~6 months  

---

## Key Responsibilities
- **Classroom Teaching:** Taught basic English to public school students (~3 hours/day), designing and delivering original 50-minute lesson plans.
- **Civic Collaboration:** Collaborated with local government officers on actionable ways to provide resources and assistance to rural youth.
- **Camp Development:** Designed youth camps focused on improving self-sufficiency, sportsmanship, and self-confidence.
- **Youth Soccer Coaching:** Coached youth soccer, teaching shooting/passing drills, and mentoring the team to victory in a local tournament.

---

## Skills Gained
- Public speaking and classroom presentation
- Cross-cultural communication and adaptability
- Rapid language acquisition and cultural immersion
- Lesson planning, mentorship, and youth leadership
